This batch validates a fundamentally new payment shape: prepaid credit ledger. This is the third payment shape category alongside money (card/cash/split) and value-trade (comp).

GOAL

Receipt indicates pack burn. Revenue books at purchase, not at burn. Pack-burn refund restores balance.
